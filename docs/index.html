How they work together

Ask the ecosystem: "Build me an AI factory."

FORGE VECTOR answers
  • Should we build it?
  • What industries need it?
  • How do we monetize it?
  • Who competes with us?
  • What if demand shifts?
NEXION answers
  • Architecture & infrastructure
  • APIs, security, scaling
  • Deployment & testing
  • Monitoring & cost
  • Production readiness
AEGIS answers
  • Does this comply with policy?
  • Who approves this change?
  • What legal obligations apply?
  • Is customer data protected?
  • Can it be audited later?

None of these opinions automatically become decisions. They feed a structured decision process where executives review the evidence and make the final call — see the Decision Pipeline.
